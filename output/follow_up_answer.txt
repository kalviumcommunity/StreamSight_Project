WHY MANUAL INVESTIGATION IS NECESSARY
======================================

A tolerance threshold only detects that two calculations are different. It does not determine which calculation is correct.

Automatically fixing a discrepancy could change a correct metric into an incorrect one.

Manual investigation is necessary to identify the actual root cause, such as NULL handling, date boundaries, timezone differences, joins, or incorrect filters.

A metric can also slowly drift while remaining below the tolerance threshold. Therefore, automated validation should detect problems, while human investigation determines the correct fix.
