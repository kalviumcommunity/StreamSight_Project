# KPI Reference Document

## KPI 1: Monthly Active Users (MAU)

Definition:
Number of unique customers who performed activity in the last 30 days.

Formula:
COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)
WHERE transaction_date >= TODAY - 30 days

Data Source:
engagement_data.csv

Columns:
customer_id, transaction_date

Target Range:
5000 - 6000

Owner:
Product Manager

Update Frequency:
Daily

Notes:
Measures customer engagement.


---

## KPI 2: Revenue Per Customer

Definition:
Average revenue generated by each customer.

Formula:

Total Revenue / Unique Customers


Data Source:
engagement_data.csv

Columns:
customer_id, amount


Target Range:
$90 - $110


Owner:
Finance Team


Frequency:
Daily


Notes:
Shows customer value.


---

## KPI 3: Churn Rate

Definition:
Percentage of customers who stop activity.

Formula:

Lost Customers / Previous Active Customers


Data Source:
transactions


Target:
0% - 5%


Owner:
Customer Success


Frequency:
Weekly


Notes:
Lower churn indicates better retention.


---

## KPI 4: Payment Success Rate

Definition:
Percentage of successful payments.

Formula:

Successful Payments / Total Payments


Target:
95% - 100%


Owner:
Payment Team


Frequency:
Daily


Notes:
Tracks payment reliability.


---

## KPI 5: Customer Acquisition Cost

Definition:
Average cost to acquire one customer.

Formula:

Marketing Cost / New Customers


Target:
$0 - $50


Owner:
Marketing Team


Frequency:
Monthly


Notes:
Measures acquisition efficiency.
